Description

Property Partners OBrien Swaine are delighted to present this superb four-bedroom family home to the market in excellent condition throughout.

No. 15 has recently been repainted throughout and fitted with new carpets, creating a bright, fresh and welcoming home ready for its new owners. The property offers generous, well laid-out accommodation including four bedrooms, three bathrooms, a spacious kitchen/dining area and a comfortable living room. Outside, there is an excellent rear garden with a patio area, providing an ideal space for outdoor dining, entertaining or family life.

The property has been very well cared for and offers an excellent opportunity for purchasers seeking a spacious family home in a highly convenient location.

Ideally positioned within this popular development, the property is within walking distance of Gorey Main Street and Esmonde Street, where an excellent selection of shops, cafes, restaurants, boutiques and other amenities can be found.

Gorey also offers an excellent choice of primary and secondary schools together with a wide range of sporting and leisure facilities including golf, GAA, rugby and soccer clubs. Some of Wexford?s finest beaches are just a short drive away, with the beautiful South East coastline within easy reach.

The location is also particularly convenient for commuters. The M11 is easily accessible via Exit 23, while Gorey Railway Station is approximately six minutes by car. A number of bus services operate from Gorey Town and South Dublin and the M50 can be reached in approximately 50 minutes.

The spacious accommodation briefly comprises entrance hall, living room, kitchen/dining room, guest WC, four bedrooms with the main bedroom en-suite, and family bathroom.

This excellent family home is sure to attract strong interest and early viewing is highly recommended.
